### 🚀 About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at **UET Lahore (2024–2028)**, building toward a career as an **Agentic AI Developer / Software Engineer** — including remote roles. I like working on systems that combine solid software engineering fundamentals with practical AI integration: full-stack apps, automation, and tools that actually reason about the task in front of them, not just respond to it.

Before software became the focus, I led the **Graphic Design Team at GCU Lahore** and worked freelance across video editing, graphic design, and social media content. That background shapes how I build — I care about clean execution and how something *feels* to use, not just whether it works.

### 🎯 Currently

- 🔭 Building and shipping full-stack projects with AI integrated in, not bolted on
- 🌱 Deepening my focus on **agentic AI systems** — LLM-driven tools that can plan and act
- 📌 Open to **Software Engineer** and **Agentic AI Developer** roles, remote-friendly
